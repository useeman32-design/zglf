About Us

Building Stronger Communities Together

Zee Glow Link Foundation (ZGLF) is a registered non-governmental organization dedicated to empowering marginalized communities across Nigeria through sustainable programs.

  • Registered NGO with headquarters in Gusau, Zamfara State
  • Four constitutional pillars: Education, Healthcare, Economic Empowerment, Humanitarian Aid
  • Transparent governance led by an elected National Executive Committee
